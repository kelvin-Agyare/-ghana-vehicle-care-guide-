Ghana Vehicle Care Guide

By Kelvin Acquah - From Lapaz, Accra

"Machines are like humans - if you treat them right, they treat you right."

1. Stop Buying Fake Parts

Fake brake pads and oil kill engines on Ghana roads. Check hologram, don't buy too cheap. Cheap is expensive.

2. Proper Repair Order

  1. Arrange - Organize tools and diagnose
  2. Wash - Clean before opening
  3. Lubricate - Oil moving parts
  4. Fix - Then repair

3. For Ghana Roads

Check tire pressure weekly (potholes)
Change oil every 5,000km due to dust
Wash under-carriage after rains

Goal: Design cars that survive Ghana roads.
